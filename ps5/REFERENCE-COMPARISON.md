# BattleStar-OSLib comparison

Downloaded reference: https://github.com/futurepr0n/BattleStar-OSLib.git
Revision: d627d007e4d521b9b89cf38a13051c4bf9851ba9
Local untouched checkout: ../../BattleStar-OSLib

## Reused

- Original Raider (42 x 20), capital ship (99 x 34), player/enemy projectile sprites and starfield layer. Originals are copied unchanged into ps5/assets and uploaded once at startup. The Viper and title artwork remain from PSPGameCPP.
- Original title music and MrDeath's 12th Moon Rising combat module. FFmpeg transcodes music for the native 48 kHz stereo mixer. The reference Viper firing WAV remains preserved in source, but version 01.003.000 replaces it with three original synthesized twin-cannon variations generated by synthesize_viper.py.
- The reference's side-scrolling combat, health, scoring and capital-ship ideas, adapted for the expanded board.

## Replaced

- Frame-randomized enemy speeds become fixed-step motion with staggered entry, formation sway, attack runs and a boss movement pattern.
- Commented-out player firing becomes repeatable twin weapon fire with a cooldown. Each side has correct collision targets and projectiles expire outside the board.
- Enemy per-frame random fire becomes per-enemy cooldowns and aimed shots. Bosses have spread fire. Enemies close to or behind the player do not start aimed attacks.
- Unsafe projectile index wrap and repeated object texture loading become bounded reusable pools and shared textures.
- Collision against dead enemies, wrong-team collision and late death handling become active-object filtering, swept collision, single-hit consumption and death at zero health.
- PSP MP3/MikMod threads and busy waiting become the existing PS5 audio mixer, two streamed Vorbis decks, crossfades and orderly shutdown.

## Left out

The PSP-only OSLib runtime, duplicate/stale movement source, compiled PSP binaries, PGF font, PSP menu prompts and fixed 480 x 272 HUD layouts are not used. The native HUD presents hull, score, wave and boss health at TV-readable sizes. The original reference checkout is preserved for comparison.

## Added

A three-sector/twelve-wave arcade run with authored formations and distinct scout, fighter, bomber and escort roles; shielded capital ships with three attack phases and warning markers; repair/shield/temporary weapon supplies; bank-directed twin cannons; muzzle flashes, hit flashes, explosion sparks and restrained camera shake; victory/defeat results and saved best scores. Between-wave repairs, damage grace, boost, pause/shell-interception handling, deterministic portable tests and package asset provenance remain. This is an initial arcade campaign; high-score write permissions, gameplay balance and PS5 performance still need console verification.
